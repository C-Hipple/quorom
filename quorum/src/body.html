Who sits in the chamber

Builders

  • A

    The Pragmatist looks for the smallest change that fits the existing code, reusing what the project already has.

  • B

    The Visionary looks for a better design than the obvious one, while keeping it buildable.

  • C

    The Architect fits it to the system's structure, with a sound data model and clean interfaces.

Council

  • The Advocate speaks for the users and checks each proposal against the requirements.

  • The Skeptic looks for what could break: regressions, security, performance and hidden complexity.

  • The Strategist weighs effort and risk against the payoff, and how the work can ship in steps.

Chair

  • The Chair counts the ranked ballots and writes the implementation plan from the winning proposal, borrowing the best of the rest.

A session makes seven requests: three proposals, three reviews and the plan. Each role runs on the agent you choose, so the builders can draft on a fast, cheap model while the council and the Chair decide on a frontier one. Inside claude.ai the agents run on Claude; opened on its own, Quorum can use OpenRouter, Hermes Agent or another OpenAI-compatible service.
